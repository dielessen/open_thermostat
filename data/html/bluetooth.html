Bluetooth sensors

Add thermometers so rooms can use them.

You pair sensors here because the E32 receives them itself over Bluetooth (radio and key). Then choose them as a room temperature source in Home Assistant or on the Rooms page.

About encryption and limits

Add a Shelly BLU H&T Display, Xiaomi Monitor 3 or Shelly BLU TRV by its Bluetooth address. Reception is local and does not need Home Assistant. These diagnostic readings cannot control heating.

Encrypted devices need their 32-character bindkey. Keys are stored on this E32 and never returned by this page. Message integrity confirms a key match; it does not confirm when the sensor measured the room. Replay protection currently lasts until restart.

Changes take effect after a manual device restart (System → Restart device). Shelly TRV control is not available in this device list yet.

TRV readings show a reported target and calculated temperature, not valve position or confirmed command feedback.

At most 8 devices, stored separately from network settings. Saving never truncates other settings. Readings expire after 15 minutes; battery-only messages do not renew temperature.
