### 0. Verdict

* **No bug-critical finding.** The safety envelope holds on every path I traced (details in 0.1).
* Three functional bugs block the "it learns and tunes itself" promise (R1-01, R1-02, R1-03). They are the rc.4 items L5, L1, L3/L4;
  section 2 has the exact anchors and a step list for each.
* The most valuable new findings are about **consistency when the plan changes** (R1-04), a **boot-zero hole** when the stored plan is
  unreadable (R1-05), an **incomplete NVS ledger** (R1-06) and the **robustness of the optimum start** before L3 widens its use (R1-07).
* The pure modules are in very good shape: monotonic 64-bit time everywhere with guarded subtraction, finite-checked restores,
  bounded JSON, allocation-failure sweeps in the soak. The modules the soak does **not** drive are `AutoTuner`, `LearnedState/Store`,
  `TakeoverReadiness`, `TakeoverTest`, `RoomOverrides`, `TrvControl`, `ConfigBackup` and the whole `HeatingController` glue
  (not host-buildable); `NightLedger`, `CurveLearner` and `BoilerMinimumFlow` run only as members of `FlowPolicy`/`FlowLearner`
  and have no invariant of their own. Most remaining risk sits there (section 3).

#### 0.1 Safety envelope (ActiveHeatGuard and everything that feeds it)

Upper bounds. A positive 0x1A can never exceed `min(plan.maximumFlowC, 80, boiler 0x16 limit)`: `ActiveHeatGuard::request()` and
`frostUnconfirmed()` cap with `cap = min(p_.maximumFlowC, hardMaximumFlowC, limit.value_or(0))` (ActiveHeatGuard.h:277,302); an unknown
limit gives `cap == 0`, so a zero is sent (`boiler_limit_unknown`). The route repeats the boiler limit and the 80 degC ceiling
(`ActiveHeatEnvelope::admits`, ActiveHeatPollDriver.h:26-29,66) and needs `heatAllowed` (plan active + confirmation). `p_.valid()` can not
turn false through `setFlowLimits`: `BoilerMinimumFlow::effective()` is at most `planMax - 1` and at least `planMin >= 20`
(BoilerMinimumFlow.h:79-82), so the guard never silently leaves `wanted` and drops the owner's confirmation.
`TrvControl::adjust` clamps to the plan range and can only cut or shift flow, never switch heat on (TrvControl.h:194-244).

"Heat while no room is below its current target" is possible only in these designed cases, none of them a hole:
(1) FlowDemand minimum-on, `min_on_s` 120..1800 s (FlowDemand.h:52, ControlTuning.h:28); (2) the hysteresis band above target,
`heat_stop_c` up to 1.0 degC (AutoTuner keeps 0.10..0.50; ZoneRegulator.h:156); (3) the optimum start, room below the COMING target (FlowPolicy.h:479-483; see R1-07); (4) frost minimum below
8 degC in any room (ActiveHeatGuard.h:299-306); (5) a held (stale) reading for up to 30 min (RoomDemand.h:54-57);
(6) the attended boiler test's +1 degC override (TakeoverTest.h:358-367). A shadow-mode plan, a closed firmware gate, a missing
confirmation, a foreign 0x1A and a not-yet-listened bus all give zero positive requests (soak `guard`/`active` scenarios assert this).
The one gap is R1-05 (boot zero not sent when the plan is unreadable).

### Lens checklist (explicit answers)

- **FreeRTOS/ISR safety.** Tasks: Arduino loop (core 1, prio 1, 8 KB; all HTTP, controller, MQTT, EMS decode, BLE queue, setup, update loop), `supervisor` (prio 5, 3 KB), `EmsBusUart` (prio 24, 4 KB), `github-ota` (prio 1, 12 KB, on demand), `OWTask` (legacy), NimBLE host (callbacks only copy into a 4-slot mutex-guarded queue), lwIP tcpip thread (DNS and route callbacks use atomics). The repo registers no ISR of its own. Shared state is atomics, mutexes or SPSC queues; read-modify-write atomics are static (internal RAM) and checked at boot. `delay` in handlers: upload `delay(1)`, FS verify `delay(0)`, `pollSetupWifi` `delay(250)` (R2-07), boot (R2-31). No logging or allocation under lock in the UART task. Issue: R2-01.
- **Heap and stack.** JSON trees, response buffer (64 KB), MQTT outbox (24 KB) and log ring (120 KB) are in PSRAM; remaining internal-heap spots are the 4 KB buffer of R2-20, `server_.arg("plain")` copies up to 4 KB, and the 12 KB updater stack. Biggest frames: R2-28.
- **Buffer bounds.** UART and telegram parsing is tight (33-byte buffer, `BreakFrameReader` drains oversize, CRC and length checks before indexing, `getValue` bounds). HTTP: head 4 KB, body 32 KB, upload 8 MiB, duplicate headers rejected. All `strcpy` sites are length-guarded. One real overflow: R2-06.
- **Auth and sessions.** 256-bit random tokens from a seeded CTR-DRBG, constant-time compares, new token at every login (no fixation), HttpOnly + SameSite=Strict + CSRF header + Origin check + Host allow-list (DNS rebinding), per-source throttling with doubling up to 15 min, factory-password gate for every non-GET, boot-local sessions. Unauthenticated routes: `/login`, `/login.js`, `/ui-language.js`, `/ui-messages.js`, `/auth-client.js`, `/auth/login`, plus the setup routes on the AP interface only. Gaps: R2-02 (public assets), R2-17, R2-19.
- **TLS for updates.** R2-11 (reset not a cert problem; bundle, hostname and time checks are correct). Authenticity: R2-10.
- **Watchdogs.** RTC watchdog 120 s is fed only by the supervisor; the 5 s task watchdog watches IDLE0 only; app OTA erases per sector with `delay(1)` per chunk; FS verify yields per sector. PBKDF2 and mounts block the loop but not the watchdogs (R2-15).
- **OTA rollback and partitions.** Rollback is armed (`verifyRollbackLater`) and confirmed only after loop, supervisor and network evidence. Problems: R2-04, R2-05, R2-25.
- **MQTT.** Outbox keeps whole frames, bounded, with control reserve; retained: `fw/hello`, availability will, telemetry. Reconnect: R2-09. Overflow edge: R2-13. Unchanged publishes: R2-16.
- **DNS/TCP timeouts.** Broker DNS (16 s) and TCP (3 s) are non-blocking. Blocking left: HTTP response writes (R2-02), PubSubClient CONNACK wait (R2-09), legacy MQTT connect (R2-23).
- **cJSON leaks.** R2-29 only.
- **Logging floods.** Syslog is capped at 10 datagrams per second with a queue of 8; the log hook uses try-lock; defaults keep chatty features off. The one steady source is R2-14.
- **What can delay the 10 s step.** R2-01, R2-02, R2-03, R2-08, R2-09, R2-15, R2-23.
- **Dead code and assets.** R2-23, R2-30. Assets are gzip-only inside the app image (44 files, about 560 KB gz of 1.2 MB raw; largest: `ems-reference.js.gz` 70 KB, `chart.umd.js.gz` 69 KB, `ui-messages-pages.js` 57 KB gz); caching: R2-24.

### What is solid

The EMS receive path is the strongest part: the UART task never logs, allocates under lock or blocks (try-lock only), frames cross to the loop through a single-producer queue, break and echo accounting is explicit, every parser checks length and CRC before indexing, and the transmit route is gated at compile time and again by finite, bounded transactions with exact reply matching. The HTTP front end bounds head, body, upload size and time before the vendor parser sees a byte, rejects ambiguous headers, and the sign-in design (random tokens, CSRF, Origin and Host checks, per-address throttling, factory-password gate, boot-local sessions) is better than most embedded products. MQTT uses non-blocking DNS and connect, a bounded outbox with a control reserve and honest stall detection, and a session-admission layer with grants and watermarks. Supervisor, RTC watchdog, black box, boot confirmation and bootloader rollback form a coherent recovery chain, OTA images are checked for size, header, board marker, SHA-256 and flash read-back, and the NVS budget is modelled and tested. JSON validators cap depth, nodes and string length, cJSON nodes go to PSRAM, and the extensive native, fuzz and browser tests show clear intent.

### 4. What is solid

- **E32 payload validation** (`heating_link.py:113-231`, `device_diagnostics.valid`): schema/type/size caps, id patterns, `number()` bounds; every decoder returns None on garbage and `receive()` ignores it. No unbounded caches: histories are capped (`MAX_SAMPLES`, `DAYS_KEPT`, `SUMMARY_DAYS_KEPT`), `learned_messages` and entity parts are pruned by the plan, `lru_cache(maxsize=4)`.
- **Availability design**: LWT plus heartbeat plus a 195 s start grace, retained heartbeats ignored, stale check every 15 s, restored without a reload.
- **Writes to the E32** (`room_plan.py`): revision-checked read-modify-write with one re-read on 409, newest-write-wins per key, waits for a free session with a Repairs issue, reauth on 401 and a one-time latch, plain error mapping (`plan_error`). `ControllerClient` is tight: fixed allowlists of paths, response size caps, local-network-only resolver, no redirects or proxies, bounded error codes, logout in `finally`.
- **Backups, firmware, diagnostics**: private store, passphrase never in errors, GitHub digest verified before upload, shared HA client session, redaction with `async_redact_data` plus regex scrub, metrics export bounded and run in the recorder executor.

### 4. The rc.3 acceptance question

**Can an acceptance receipt for rc.3, captured only AFTER its install, pass `verify_e32_update_gate.py`, and can
`verify_accepted_update_path.py` validate it?**

Mechanically yes, honestly only partly. `METHODS['home_assistant']` (`home_assistant_acceptance`, gate lines 110-178)
checks field presence and internal consistency and has no notion of when evidence was captured. I built a rc.3
receipt from the real published values plus a post-install-only reading (observations two days after the install):
`verify()` returned PASS (`publication_basis: accepted_previous_release`) for candidate rc.4 with `accepted` = that
receipt, and `verify_accepted_update_path.accepted_source()` returned it. `verify_accepted_update_path.py` is not a
receipt validator: line 81 re-runs the same gate, then it uses only `record['tag']` (must be beta or later) and
`record['source_commit']` (for `git show <commit>:<retained updater file>`, here 666daf68, already fetched by the CI
step at build.yml:306) to compile the retained updater against the CANDIDATE image. Each pre-install item the gate
demands still blocks when absent (simulated): `backup_verified` false -> "Incomplete physical update acceptance";
no `ha_installed_version_before` -> "HA installed version before is not older"; `ha_latest_version_before != tag` ->
"HA did not offer this release"; installer hash equal to independent hash -> "Independent check must be a separate
receipt"; one observation -> "Missing boot observations"; observation before `finished_at`; last uptime < 600.
