Managed in Home Assistant
Where do I set what?
  1. Home AssistantThe main place: rooms, temperature sources, target temperatures and week programs.
  2. Room plan on the E32Stored on the E32 and kept in step with Home Assistant. It keeps running without Home Assistant.
  3. SensorsThermostats and other devices come in through Home Assistant; Bluetooth sensors are received by the E32 itself.
  4. BoilerIn shadow mode the E32 only calculates. It controls the boiler only after the attended switch-over.

When Home Assistant is offline, the E32 continues with its own Bluetooth sensors and week programs; otherwise it keeps the temperature without Home Assistant.

Active control

Lets the E32 control the boiler itself instead of your thermostat, after an attended switch-over.

Active control is not enabled in this firmware.
