Boiler test and takeover

The recommended way to let the E32 control your boiler. The E32 checks every step itself from what the boiler reports. Stay near the boiler; it takes about 30 minutes.

Show the steps
    Stop and give control back to your thermostat:
    1. Press “Give control back to my thermostat”, or switch the boiler off.
    2. Switch the boiler off, reconnect your old thermostat to the boiler and switch the boiler back on.
    3. Check hot water and the water pressure. Start the test again once the cause is fixed.

    Or choose another way:

    A trial run is for a home that still has its old thermostat on the boiler: for a few days the E32 only calculates and compares with it. You can start the boiler test at any time.
