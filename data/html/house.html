Water temperature limits

The lowest and highest temperature of the water the boiler sends to the radiators and floor (flow temperature), for all rooms together. The boiler's own maximum always applies as well.

A room has floor heating. Floor heating fed directly without a mixing valve usually needs at most 45–50 °C.

Radiators often need 60–75 °C on cold days. Floor heating fed directly without a mixing valve usually needs at most 45–50 °C.
