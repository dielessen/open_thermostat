About this project

OpenThermostat

Independent E32 V2 firmware & Home Assistant integration.

In development & testing

Developed by Mark Dielessen

OpenThermostat is being independently developed into a local heating system, with Home Assistant for room configuration and connected devices, and the E32 V2 as the control core.

The control architecture, Home Assistant integration, room settings, weekly planner and user interface are being extensively developed and reworked for this project. Development, maintenance and releases are managed independently.
