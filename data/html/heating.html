Where do I set what?
  1. Without Home AssistantSet everything on this page: rooms, Bluetooth sensors and week programs work on the E32 alone.
  2. Home Assistant (optional)When you use it, it is the main place: rooms, temperature sources, target temperatures and week programs.
  3. Room plan on the E32Stored on the E32 and kept in step with Home Assistant. It keeps running without Home Assistant.
  4. BoilerIn shadow mode the E32 only calculates. It controls the boiler only after the attended switch-over.

When Home Assistant is offline, the E32 continues with its own Bluetooth sensors and week programs; otherwise it keeps the temperature without Home Assistant.
