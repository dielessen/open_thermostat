Connect to your WiFi

Choose your 2.4 GHz network or enter the name manually. The setup network remains available while trying. Settings are only saved after a successful connection.

You can also enter a hidden network manually.

WiFi connected

Open OpenThermostat on your home network

Next steps

  1. Sign in. The first time: user admin, password admin. Then choose your own password under System → About.
  2. Optional: connect Home Assistant (Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → OpenThermostat).
  3. Add your first room on the Rooms page. Without Home Assistant everything is set there.
Your phone or laptop must be connected to the same home network. If this does not happen automatically, select your home WiFi. If the sign-in window closes automatically, open this address in your regular browser. You can save it as a bookmark.
